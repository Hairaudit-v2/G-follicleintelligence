## Discovery (why not the full hot cluster)

The deferred HR / role-hours surface mixes:

| Concern | Files | Owner |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Clinical role bookability / needs_review | `staffRolePolicy` | **directory** (this slice) |
| HR portal notification DTO / loader | `staffHrNotification*` | **not directory** — identity readiness consumes stale-day constants / summary contracts |
| Employment weekly hours + booking slot gate | `staffWeeklyHours`, `staffSlotHours` | deferred — separate employment vs roster vs booking semantics |
| Payroll import role-review workflow | `staffRoleReview*` | deferred — composition over payroll + clinic + HR notification |
| My HR portal selection/loader | `myHrPortal*` | deferred with notification ownership |

Moving those as one cluster would force product-policy decisions and risk `team/identity` → `team/directory` imports.
