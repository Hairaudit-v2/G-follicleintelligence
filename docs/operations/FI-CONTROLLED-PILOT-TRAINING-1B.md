# FI-CONTROLLED-PILOT-TRAINING-1B

**Phase:** `FI-CONTROLLED-PILOT-ACTIVATION-1B`  
**Pathway:** quote_to_deposit  

## Training must cover

1. Control Centre purpose (observe, do not treat as clinical SoR)  
2. Pilot scope and exclusions (no Stripe, no bulk invite, Evolved only)  
3. Health banner interpretation (GREEN/AMBER/RED; empty cohort ≠ success)  
4. Readiness states and fail-closed unknowns  
5. Blocker severity, ownership, ageing, escalation  
6. Critical stop conditions and pause procedure  
7. Role permissions (what each role can/cannot see)  
8. Sensitive information handling  
9. Manual fallback recording (no PHI in telemetry)  
10. Incident reporting  
11. Patient communication boundaries  
12. Pilot pause / withdrawal
